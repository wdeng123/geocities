1. My Putter Collection

  • It started in 1989 with one blade putter from a yard sale for 50 cents. Now there are 23 of them leaning in a golf bag by the furnace.
  • My favorite is a heavy old mallet with a brass face. It rolls the ball like butter on fast greens.
  • The worst one? A homemade job with a doorknob for a head. It stays in the collection for laughs.
  • Hobbyist tip: swap-meets and church rummage sales are GOLD MINES for old flatsticks!

2. Regrip a Club for Under Five Bucks

  1. Lock the shaft in a vise with a rubber shaft clamp so you don't crack the finish.
  2. Slice the old grip off with a hook blade, cutting AWAY from your hand. Safety first!
  3. Peel off the old tape. A little mineral spirits helps the stubborn stuff.
  4. Wrap on fresh two-sided tape, wet it with solvent, and slide the new grip on in one push.
  5. Line up the logo, stand it in the corner overnight, and you are ready to rip!

3. Bucket-of-Balls Practice Plans

  • The 30-Minute Lunch Bucket: 10 wedges, 10 seven-irons, 10 drivers, then 15 minutes of chipping.
  • The Ladder: Hit to the 50, 75, 100 and 125 yard flags in order. Miss one? Start over!
  • The Pretend Round: Play your home course in your head, one ball per shot, and keep score honest.
